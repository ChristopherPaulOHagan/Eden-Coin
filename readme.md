# Eden Coin (EDN) — Core Architecture & Legal Framework

An open-source, distributed, planetary restoration ledger designed to anchor computational inference to physical earth assets. Eden Coin strips away speculative tokenomics and corporate hoarding protocols, creating a closed-loop system where value directly scales with verifiable environmental restoration.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy & Mandate

Eden Coin rejects traditional speculative market mechanics, platform decay (enshittification), and political centralization. 
* Language, code, and financial value within this network exist strictly for **description, utility, and execution**—never for corporate domination or manipulation.
* The system infrastructure uses decentralized computation and planetary telemetry (via Google Earth Engine) to serve the physical Earth directly.

---

## 2. Tokenomics & Velocity Controls (The Non-Hoarding Protocols)

To prevent the standard lifecycle of platform decay caused by wealth concentration, the protocol enforces strict mathematical constraints on token circulation and utility.

### A. The Velocity Limit & Expiration Loop
* **No Permanent Capital Accumulation:** Tokens are structural utility instruments, not speculative investments. 
* **The 3-to-5-Year Expiration Window:** Every token issued carries an embedded temporal decay parameter. If a token remains stagnant without being redeployed into verified physical restoration or local node infrastructure within a **3 to 5-year cycle**, it is automatically burned by the ledger sentinel layer and recycled back into the global emission pool.

### B. Issuance Limits & Capping Metrics
* **The Monthly Cap:** Individual human node operations are hard-capped at a max velocity of **10 trees/milestones per month** to prevent corporate farming and industrial exploitation.
* **The Flexible Pension Cap:** Payout distribution targets maximum real-world utility value, scaling up to a flexible pension baseline ($1,000 USD equivalent milestone valuation) for verified, long-term environmental custody.
